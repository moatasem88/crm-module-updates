What the system does for you

Eight automations, and what sets each one off

Every one of these fires on save — nothing waits for a nightly job, and nothing needs a button pressed afterwards. The dates are stamped once, on the save that moves the status, and never change afterwards. One gate goes with the last row: the pop-up that asks which tender was approved offers only tenders that are Submitted.

Running quietly in the background

The parts of each record that fill themselves

Nothing here is yours to keep up to date. The blue blocks are rebuilt by the system every time something they depend on is saved — and the dates among them are written once, the day each status is reached.

The other half of the picture

The line the automation does not cross

Four statuses are set for you — the four on the right. Every other status in the system is a choice somebody makes.

Read the direction of travel. You never create a Customer or a Project Tender yourself, and you never set an Activity to Done because a tender went out. Fill the record in front of you, save it, and the next record appears.

Step 1 of 9 · you

Create the Lead

FILL IN

Who the enquiry is from

Lead Name and Organization, plus the contacts in the Lead's own contacts table.

FILL IN — DO NOT SKIP

Customer Group and Territory

The Customer is built from these two fields later. Leave them empty and the conversion in step 3 fails.

LEAVE ALONE

Qualification Status

Starts at Unqualified. Your Activities move it — you never set it by hand.

If you skip Customer Group or Territory the Registration Activity in step 3 stops with “Cannot convert Lead … to a Customer: no Customer Group and Territory.” Set them on the Lead, save it, then set the Activity to Done again.

Step 2 of 9 · you

Log an Activity — the record that drives everything

Open the Lead and use Create → Activity, or New Activity on the Activities tab. Two fields decide what the system does next.

THE FIELD THAT DECIDES

Type

Call Meeting Site Visit Registration Tendering Other

Registration creates the Customer. Tendering creates the Project Tender. The other four are just a record of the contact.

THE FIELD THAT FIRES IT

Status

Planned In Process Done Failed Closed Waiting Reply

Defaults to Planned. Done is the trigger — nothing else in this list sets anything off.

THE REST

Date, Assigned To, Link Type, Link, Attachments

Link Type is Lead, Prospect or Opportunity, and Link is the record itself. Both are filled for you when you use the buttons above.

The rule that catches everyone first

No attachment, no save.

Every Activity on a Lead or an Opportunity needs at least one file in Attachments, whatever its Type. A Registration Activity needs one wherever it is linked — it is the paperwork the Customer is created from. The Opportunity itself will not save without an attachment either.

An attachment row with no file in it does not count. If the message says مرفق مطلوب (attachment required), you have an empty row, not a file.

Step 3 of 9 · you set the status, the system converts

Registration turns the Lead into a Customer

YOU — ON CREATION

Save a Type = Registration Activity on the Lead

The Lead's Qualification Status moves to In Process. This happens once, when the Activity is first created.

YOU — WHEN IT IS SETTLED

Set that Activity's Status to Done

Attach the registration document first, then save. This is the only action in the whole stage that converts anything.

THE SYSTEM — ON THAT SAVE

Customer created, Lead Converted

Qualification Status becomes Qualified, the Customer is created from the Lead with its contacts and addresses, and a comment on the Lead records who did it.

Two separate effects, easy to confuse. Any Activity reaching Done marks the Lead Qualified. Only a Registration Activity reaching Done also creates the Customer. Re-saving it never creates a second Customer.

Six statuses, one of them with consequences

Statuses are a plain dropdown: any of them can be chosen at any time. Nothing except Done starts an automation.

Step 4 of 9 · you

Open the Opportunity on the Customer

WHERE

On the Customer, not the Lead

Opportunity From = Customer and Party Name = the Customer created in step 3.

FILL IN

Targeted Amount and Opportunity Breakdown

Each breakdown row takes a Contribution Type of Percentage or Fixed Amount; Final Value is worked out for you as you type.

FILL IN

At least one Attachment

The Opportunity follows the same rule as an Activity: it will not save empty-handed.

THEN

Log Activities here too

Same buttons, same rules. Link Type is now Opportunity, and the Opportunity keeps its own Activities table.

Contact Person lists the Opportunity's own contacts, not everyone attached to the Customer. Add the contact on the Opportunity if you cannot find them.

Step 5 of 9 · you log it, the system builds it

A Tendering Activity creates the Project Tender

YOU

Activity with Type = Tendering on the Opportunity

Attach the invitation to tender, then Save. The Status can still be Planned — it is the save that builds the tender, not the status.

THE SYSTEM — ON THAT SAVE

One Project Tender, named after the Opportunity

Project Name = the Opportunity's Title, Client = the Customer, and Opportunity + Leads Activity point back to where it came from.

THE SYSTEM — AT THE SAME TIME

Opportunity Status → Tendering

A green message gives you the link to the new Project Tender, and it is listed on the Opportunity's Project Tender tab.

One Activity, one Project Tender. Re-saving the Activity, or switching its Type away and back, never creates a second one. Need a second tender on the same Opportunity? Log a second Tendering Activity.

Step 6 of 9 · you and the estimation team

Cost the Project Tender

TABLE 1

Direct Cost Details

One row per Tender — the trade packages this price is built from. No Tender may appear twice.

TABLE 2

Indirect Cost Details

Site overheads and everything else that is not a trade package.

TABLE 3

Overhead / Deduction Details

Overhead, profit and deductions, as a percentage or an amount.

AND IN EACH TENDER

Press Mark Complete

Each Tender runs Draft → In Progress → Complete. Until it is Complete, its BOQ can still move the Project Tender's totals.

This is the work the next slide checks. All three tables need at least one row, and every Tender in Direct Cost Details must be Complete, before the Project Tender can leave the office.

The decision point · where most errors happen

Submitted and Won are gated.

A Project Tender will refuse to save into either status until both conditions hold:

1 · Every Tender in Direct Cost Details is Complete.
Error: “Linked Tender not Complete”, naming the row and the status it is in. 2 · All three cost tables have at least one row (for Submitted).
Error: Project Tender غير مكتمل — naming the empty tables.

The fix is always the same: open each Tender it names, press Mark Complete — or fill the table it names — then set the status again. Rows whose win has already been processed are left alone, so re-saving a Won tender never re-opens settled work.

Step 7 of 9 · you set the status, the system closes the Activity

Submitted sends the price out

YOU

Project Tender Status → Submitted

Set it and save. If the gate stops you, read the message: it names exactly which Tender or which table is holding you up.

THE SYSTEM

The Tendering Activity is marked Done

The Activity that created this tender closes itself, so the Opportunity's Activities table shows the tendering step as finished.

DELIBERATELY NOT TOUCHED

The Opportunity's Status

It stays Tendering. Which tender was actually approved is decided the other way round — from the Opportunity, in step 8.

From here the tendering stage runs. The price is with the client, and the Project Tender waits in Submitted until there is an answer.

Lost

The client awarded the work elsewhere.

Cancelled

Dropped before the price went out. A cancelled Tender is also taken out of Direct Cost Details.

Superseded

Replaced by a newer Project Tender for the same Opportunity.

Steps 8 and 9 of 9 · the pop-up

Won asks which Project Tender you relied on

1 · YOU

Opportunity Status → Won

A pop-up opens, asking which Project Tender was approved:

أي Project Tender تم اعتماده؟

2 · YOU

Pick it, then press اعتماد (Approve)

The list shows this Opportunity's tenders that are Submitted — and only those — each as name · project · status · value. Your choice lands in the read-only Approved Project Tender field.

3 · YOU — THE STEP PEOPLE FORGET

Save the Opportunity

Approving in the pop-up only fills the field. Nothing reaches the Project Tender until the Opportunity is saved.

4 · THE SYSTEM

That one tender → Won

Its own Won automation then raises the Project and the Sales Order. The other tenders on the Opportunity are left exactly as they are.

Close the pop-up without choosing — Esc or the ✕ — and the Status goes back to what it was. An Opportunity is never left Won with no approved tender behind it.

Nothing in the list? The message says which case it is: no tenders at all — the direct-award case — or tenders that are none of them Submitted yet. A price the client never saw cannot be the one they approved. Either way the status stands.

If the win is refused — a Tender not Complete, a Tender with no Client — the whole save rolls back, Opportunity included. Fix what the message names and save again.

WATCH FOR

An Opportunity already Won

Creating a Tendering Activity on it does not push it back to Tendering. Re-saving a Won Opportunity with the same approved tender does nothing either — the win is processed once.

Quick reference · keep this slide

Nine things you do. Everything else is done for you.

  1. 1Create the Lead — with Customer Group and Territory filled in.
  2. 2Log Activities on it. Always attach a file.
  3. 3Set the Registration Activity to Done → auto the Customer appears.
  4. 4Create the Opportunity on that Customer, with an attachment.
  5. 5Save an Activity with Type = Tendering → auto the Project Tender appears and the Opportunity goes Tendering.
  6. 6Fill all three cost tables, and Mark Complete every linked Tender.
  7. 7Project Tender → Submitted → auto the Tendering Activity closes.
  8. 8Opportunity → Won, pick the approved tender in the pop-up, press اعتماد.
  9. 9Save the Opportunity → auto that tender goes Won, and its Project and Sales Order are raised.
  10. !Blocked at step 7 or 8? A Tender is not Complete, or a cost table is empty. The message names it.

The one habit that prevents most problems: fill the record in front of you, attach the paperwork, and save. Do not reach into the next record to set a status that the system is going to set for you.
